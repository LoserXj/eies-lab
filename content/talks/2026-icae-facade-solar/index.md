abstract: |
  Facades offer far more surface than roofs in dense cities, yet most urban solar assessments stop at
  the rooftop. This talk combines street-view facade parsing, 3D building models and hourly meteorology
  to estimate facade PV potential building by building, and shows how shading from neighbouring
  buildings changes the result compared with rooftop-only estimates.
